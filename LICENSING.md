[PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

The [PLwC Personal Use Permission 1.0](PERSONAL_USE_PERMISSION.md) additionally
permits individuals to use and modify PLwC for their own private and personal
purposes.

The additional [PLwC Official Redistribution Permission
1.0](OFFICIAL_REDISTRIBUTION_PERMISSION.md) allows exact, complete and
unmodified Official Releases to be shared free of charge. It does not permit
publication or redistribution of modified versions.

## Commercial and special versions

Commercial distribution, official custom versions, OEM or embedded use,
white-label use, paid hosting and managed services require a separate written
agreement. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md).

## Previously published Apache-2.0 material

Copies and versions that were already lawfully published under the Apache
License 2.0 remain available to their recipients under those terms. Those
rights are not revoked or restricted by the PLwC Personal license. The
historical license text is preserved at
[LICENSES/APACHE-2.0.txt](LICENSES/APACHE-2.0.txt).

The presence of an Apache-licensed historical version does not make later
PLwC Personal changes available under Apache 2.0.

## Third-party material

Third-party components remain under their own licenses. In particular, the
PLwC Chat Bridge records the MIT-licensed MCP SuperAssistant baseline and its
required notice under
`integrations/plwc-chat-bridge/LICENSES/MCP-SuperAssistant-MIT.txt`.

Nothing in the PLwC Personal license limits rights granted directly by a
third-party licensor for third-party material. Binary releases must preserve
all applicable notices shipped with their dependencies.

## Public integration specifications

No current PLwC Personal Core file is automatically licensed as an open
integration specification. A protocol specification or connector SDK becomes
openly licensed only when it is placed in a separately identified directory
with its own explicit Apache-2.0 or MIT license notice.
